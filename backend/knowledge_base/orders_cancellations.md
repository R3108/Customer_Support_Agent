# Orders, Changes & Cancellations

## Order statuses explained
- **Processing**: we received your order and are preparing it. It can still be changed or canceled.
- **Shipped**: the package has left our warehouse and has a tracking number.
- **Out for delivery**: the carrier expects to deliver it today.
- **Delivered**: the carrier marked the package as delivered.
- **Canceled**: the order was canceled and any authorization has been released.
- **Return in progress**: a return has been started for one or more items.
- **Refunded**: a refund has been issued to the original payment method.

## Canceling an order
You can cancel an order yourself from the Orders page while its status is "processing", usually within about 1 hour of placing it. Once an order has shipped it can't be canceled, but you can return it for free by refusing delivery or starting a return after it arrives.

## Changing an order
While an order is "processing" you can change the size or color of an item, or remove an item. Items can't be added to an existing order; please place a new order instead. Promo codes can't be added after checkout.

## Order confirmation not received
Order confirmation emails are sent within 15 minutes. Check your spam folder and confirm the email address on your account. If you checked out as a guest, you can look up the order using your email address and order number.

## Backorders and pre-orders
Backordered items show an estimated ship date on the product page and in your confirmation email. You are charged only when the item ships. You can cancel a backordered item at any time before it ships.

## Split shipments
Orders with items from different warehouses may arrive in separate packages, each with its own tracking number. You are never charged extra shipping for a split shipment.

## Order number format
Aurora order numbers start with "ORD-" followed by five digits, for example ORD-10421. You can find your order number in your confirmation email or on the Orders page of your account.
