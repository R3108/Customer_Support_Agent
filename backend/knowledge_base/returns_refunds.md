# Returns, Exchanges & Refunds

## Return window
Most items can be returned within **60 days of delivery** for a full refund. Items must be unused, clean, and in original packaging with tags attached. Aurora+ members get an extended **90-day** return window.

## Items that cannot be returned
The following items are final sale: gift cards, personalized or engraved products, opened fuel canisters, opened food and nutrition products, and items marked "Final Sale" on the product page. Used climbing ropes and harnesses cannot be returned for safety reasons unless they are defective.

## How to start a return
1. Go to Orders in your account and select "Start a return" next to the item.
2. Choose a reason and whether you want a refund or an exchange.
3. Print the prepaid return label, or choose "QR code" to drop off without printing at any UPS Store.
Returns are free for exchanges and for defective items. For other refunds a $5.95 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund, which is waived for Aurora+ members.

## Refund timing
Once the warehouse receives your return, refunds are processed within 3 business days. Refunds go back to the original payment method. Banks typically take 5–10 business days to post the credit. Refunds to Aurora gift cards are instant.

## Refund approval limits
The support assistant can confirm eligibility and explain the return process. Refunds or goodwill credits **over $250**, refunds outside the return window, and refunds for orders without a return shipment always require approval by a human support specialist.

## Exchanges
Exchanges for a different size or color are free. We ship the replacement as soon as the return is scanned by the carrier, so you don't have to wait for the warehouse to receive it. If the price of the new item is different, we refund or charge the difference.

## Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged or develops a defect, contact support with your order number and a photo of the damage. We cover return shipping and send a replacement or full refund. Damaged-item claims are reviewed by a human specialist, usually within one business day.

## Wrong item received
If you received the wrong item, we will send the correct item via Expedited Shipping at no cost and provide a prepaid label to return the incorrect item. Wrong-item reports are routed to a specialist.

## Price adjustments
If an item you bought drops in price within 14 days of purchase, we will refund the difference once per item. Price adjustments do not apply to clearance, flash sales, or competitor prices.
