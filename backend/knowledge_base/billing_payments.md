# Billing & Payments

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Aurora gift cards, and Klarna (pay in 4 interest-free installments on orders from $50 to $1,500).

## When you are charged
Your card is authorized when you place an order and charged when the order ships. If an order ships in multiple packages, you may see multiple charges that add up to the order total. Pending authorizations for canceled orders drop off within 3–7 business days depending on your bank.

## Duplicate or unexpected charges
A pending authorization and a final charge can look like a duplicate charge; the authorization disappears within a few days. If you see two completed charges for the same order, or a charge you don't recognize, contact support. Unrecognized charges and suspected fraud are handled by a human billing specialist as a high priority.

## Sales tax
Sales tax is calculated based on the shipping address and applicable state and local rates. Tax-exempt organizations can apply for an exemption by emailing their certificate to tax@auroraoutfitters.example.

## Invoices and receipts
Receipts are emailed when an order ships. You can download a PDF invoice from the Orders page of your account at any time. Business customers can request invoices with a VAT or company ID added.

## Promo codes and discounts
Only one promo code can be used per order. Promo codes cannot be applied after an order has been placed, cannot be combined with Aurora+ member pricing on the same item, and cannot be used to purchase gift cards. If a code is not working, check the expiration date and minimum order amount.

## Gift cards
Aurora gift cards never expire and have no fees. Check your balance on the Gift Cards page. Lost gift cards can be replaced only if you have the original order number or the card number.

## Chargebacks and disputes
If you have filed or are considering a chargeback with your bank, please contact us first — we can usually resolve the issue faster. Any conversation mentioning a chargeback or dispute is escalated to a billing specialist.
