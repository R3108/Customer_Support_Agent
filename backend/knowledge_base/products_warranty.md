# Products, Sizing & Warranty

## Aurora lifetime warranty
Aurora-brand products (tents, packs, sleeping bags, and apparel) are covered by our lifetime warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, accidents, misuse, or damage from improper care. Third-party brands are covered by their manufacturer's warranty.

## Making a warranty claim
Submit a warranty claim with your order number (or proof of purchase), a description of the problem, and photos. Our gear specialists review claims within 5 business days and will repair, replace, or issue store credit. Warranty claims are reviewed by a human specialist.

## Sizing help
Our apparel follows standard US sizing. Each product page has a size chart with chest, waist, hip, and inseam measurements. If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up for insulated jackets and layering pieces, and sizing down for base layers that should fit snugly. Hiking boots run true to size; order a half size up if you plan to wear thick socks.

## Tent care
Always dry your tent completely before storing it to prevent mildew. Clean with lukewarm water and a non-detergent soap. Never machine wash or tumble dry a tent. Seam sealing is recommended every 2–3 seasons for heavy use.

## Sleeping bag temperature ratings
Aurora sleeping bags list a comfort rating and a limit rating based on the ISO 23537 standard. The comfort rating is the temperature at which a typical cold sleeper stays comfortable. Choose a bag with a comfort rating at least 10°F below the lowest temperature you expect.

## Down product care
Wash down jackets and sleeping bags in a front-loading machine using a down-specific wash on a gentle cycle. Tumble dry on low with two or three clean tennis balls to restore loft. Store sleeping bags loosely in the large storage sack, not compressed.

## Product availability and restocks
Out-of-stock items show a "Notify me" button on the product page. We can't guarantee restock dates, but most core items restock within 4–6 weeks. Seasonal and limited-edition items may not return.

## Product recalls and safety
If a product is subject to a safety recall, we notify affected customers by email and post details on our Safety Notices page. Any report of injury or a safety hazard related to a product is escalated immediately to a human specialist.
