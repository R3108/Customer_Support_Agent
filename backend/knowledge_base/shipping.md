# Shipping & Delivery

## Shipping options and delivery times
Aurora Outfitters ships from warehouses in Reno, NV and Columbus, OH. Orders placed before 2:00 PM Eastern on a business day ship the same day.
- **Standard Shipping** (free on orders over $75, otherwise $6.95): 3–7 business days.
- **Expedited Shipping** ($14.95): 2–3 business days.
- **Overnight Shipping** ($29.95): next business day when ordered before 12:00 PM Eastern.
Delivery estimates start from the day the order ships, not the day it is placed. Weekends and US federal holidays are not business days.

## Where we ship
We ship to all 50 US states, Puerto Rico, APO/FPO/DPO military addresses, and Canada. Overnight Shipping is only available in the contiguous United States. We do not currently ship to other international destinations.

## International and Canada shipping
Canadian orders ship via Canada Post or UPS Worldwide and typically arrive in 6–10 business days. Duties and taxes are calculated and collected at checkout, so there are no surprise fees on delivery. Oversized items such as kayaks and canoes cannot ship to Canada.

## Tracking your shipment
When your order ships you receive an email with a tracking number. You can also see tracking on the Orders page of your account. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show movement after the label is created. The Relay support assistant can look up the live status of any order if you share your order number (for example ORD-10421).

## Delayed or lost packages
If your tracking has not updated for 5 business days, or your package is more than 3 business days past its estimated delivery date, we treat it as delayed. We will open a trace with the carrier. If the carrier confirms the package is lost, we ship a free replacement using Expedited Shipping or issue a full refund, whichever you prefer.

## Package marked delivered but not received
First check around your property, with neighbors, and your building's mailroom. Carriers sometimes mark packages delivered up to 48 hours early. If the package still has not arrived 48 hours after being marked delivered, contact support and a specialist will open a claim. Missing-package claims are always handled by a human specialist.

## Changing a shipping address
You can change the shipping address while the order status is "processing". Once the order status is "shipped", the address can no longer be changed by us, but you can request a delivery change directly with the carrier using the tracking number.

## Oversized and freight items
Kayaks, canoes, and roof cargo boxes ship via freight. Freight deliveries require a scheduled appointment and a signature, and take 7–14 business days. A $49 freight surcharge applies regardless of order total.
