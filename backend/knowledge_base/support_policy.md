# Contact & Support Policy

## Support hours
The Relay AI assistant is available 24/7. Human support specialists are available Monday–Friday 6:00 AM–9:00 PM Eastern and Saturday–Sunday 8:00 AM–6:00 PM Eastern.

## Talking to a human
You can ask to speak with a human at any time and the assistant will hand the conversation to a specialist together with a summary, so you don't have to repeat yourself. Specialist replies appear in the same chat window.

## Response time targets
- **Urgent** (account compromise, safety issues, fraud): within 1 hour
- **High** (billing disputes, lost or damaged orders): within 4 business hours
- **Normal** (returns, warranty, general account help): within 1 business day
- **Low** (feedback, product suggestions): within 2 business days

## Other ways to contact us
- Email: help@auroraoutfitters.example
- Phone: 1-800-555-0199 (during specialist hours)
- Mail: Aurora Outfitters Customer Care, 1200 Summit Way, Reno, NV 89501

## Privacy of your conversation
Conversations are stored securely to help specialists assist you and to improve our service. We never ask for your full card number, password, or 2FA codes in chat. If anyone asks for these, it isn't us.

## Feedback
We love hearing from you. After a conversation you can rate your experience. Product ideas and general feedback are shared with the relevant team every week.

## Store locations
Aurora Outfitters has retail stores in Reno NV, Boulder CO, Portland OR, and Burlington VT. Online orders can be returned in any store. Store hours are 10:00 AM–8:00 PM local time, 7 days a week.
