# Account, Security & Aurora+ Membership

## Resetting your password
Select "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter your account email. A reset link valid for 30 minutes is sent to that email. If you don't see it, check your spam folder. For your security, support agents can never see or set your password.

## Updating account details
You can update your name, email address, phone number, and saved addresses under Account Settings. Changing your email requires confirming the change from both the old and new email addresses.

## Two-factor authentication
We strongly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA) under Account Settings → Security. We support authenticator apps and SMS codes. If you lose access to your 2FA device, use one of your backup codes or contact support for identity verification, which is handled by a human specialist.

## Suspected account compromise
If you notice orders you didn't place, changed addresses, or a password you didn't change, reset your password immediately and contact support. Suspected account takeovers are treated as urgent and escalated to our trust & safety team.

## Deleting your account
You can request account deletion under Account Settings → Privacy. Deletion is completed within 30 days. Open orders and pending returns must be completed first. Order records required for tax purposes are retained as required by law.

## Aurora+ membership
Aurora+ is our membership program for $49 per year. Benefits include:
- Free Expedited Shipping on every order
- 90-day return window and free return shipping
- 10% member pricing on full-price items
- Early access to seasonal sales
- Double rewards points
Membership renews automatically each year. You can cancel auto-renew anytime under Account Settings → Membership.

## Canceling Aurora+
If you cancel within 30 days of joining or renewing and have not used any member benefits, you receive a full refund. After 30 days, you keep benefits until the end of the membership year and are not charged again.

## Rewards points
Everyone earns 1 point per $1 spent (Aurora+ members earn 2). Every 500 points converts into a $10 reward. Rewards expire 12 months after they are issued. Points are added once the return window for the order has closed.
